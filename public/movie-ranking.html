Best TV series according to Abhishek

Prison Break is a gripping and adrenaline-thrilled television series that kept me on the edge of my seat

Season 1:

  • Michael, a structural engineer, fakes a bank robbery to get himself sent to Fox River State Penitentiary, the same prison his brother Lincoln is incarcerated in, to orchestrate Lincoln's escape.
  • Michael uses his knowledge of the prison's design and his tattoo, which contains a map of the prison, to help Lincoln escape.
  • They escape along with six other inmates, forming the "Fox River Eight".

Season 2:

  • The escapees are on the run and are hunted by law enforcement, including the aggressive Captain Bellick.
  • Lincoln, Michael, and the others navigate the manhunt, facing threats and betrayals while trying to clear Lincoln's name and expose the conspiracy that led to his wrongful conviction.
  • Key characters like Sara Tancredi and Alexander Mahone are also on the run, adding to the complexity of the situation.
